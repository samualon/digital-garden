# Motivation
Valuation of a firm is a difficult task, but crucial to know when negotiating. It's difficult because:
- Uncertainty
- Lack of value-relevant information
- Asymmetric information
- Difficult to value intangible assets, such as [[human capital]].

For this reason, traditional valuation methods aren't applicable.
# Main approaches
Four main approaches exist for entrepreneurial firm valuation:
- [[Venture capital model]]
- [[Discounted cash flow model]]
- [[Comparables model]]
- [[Probalistic model]] with model uncertainty
