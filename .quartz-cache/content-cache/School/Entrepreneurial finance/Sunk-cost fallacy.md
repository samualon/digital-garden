When an individual persists in their behavior, because they've already committed time or money to something.

In investing, the [[Sunk-cost fallacy]] can influence investors to keep investing, even after multiple negative results, simply because of the fact that they've already invested money into the business.
# Countering
- See [[Sunk cost]]s as [[Sunk cost]]s and re-evaluate every time if new investment is needed. Is it still worth it?
- Be prepared to kill projects early.
- Use [[Gated funding]]
