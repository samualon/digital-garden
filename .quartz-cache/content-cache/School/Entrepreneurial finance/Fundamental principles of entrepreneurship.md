Entrepreneurship can be characterized using three fundamental principles:
1. Gathering and recombining resources
2. Uncertainty
3. Experimentation
# Gathering and recombining resources
Entrepreneurs need to convince owners to give (financial) resources.
# Uncertainty
Unlike risk, where potential outcomes are know, uncertainty entails a situation where outcomes and probabilities of these outcomes are unknown.

When outcomes are known and we talk about risk, it's no longer entrepreneurial but managerial.
# Experimentation
Entrepreneurship entails exploration, as apposed to established firms that focus on exploitation.

This exploration requires an organizational structure that fosters dynamic flexibility. They need to be able to *pivot*.
