This expresses the difference between a companies equity and long-term liabilities and its fixed assets:
$$ WC = Equity - LT\:loans - fixed \: assets $$
It represents the cash needed to sustain current operational activities and depends on the cash flows of the company.
# Meaning
A healthy state is assumed when $WC>0$.

The [[Working Capital]] can be compared against the [[Working capital requirement]]. If $WCR>WC$, then short-term loans should be taken.
