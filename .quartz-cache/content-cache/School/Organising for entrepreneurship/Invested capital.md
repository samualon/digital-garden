> *"Invested capital represents the cumulative amount the business has invested in its core operations; primarily property, plant, and equipment and working capital"*
> **Koller**

$$ Invested \: capital = Fixed \: assets + Inventory + Accounts \: receivable - Current \: liabilities $$

With $Fixed \: assets =$ property, plants and equipment. Or:
$$ Invested \: capital = Equity + Non \: current \: liabilities - Cash $$
