# External financing
## Borrower commitment
Banks care about collateral even though collateral doesn't impact success rate of the borrower's projects. The commitment of the borrower is important.

Borrowers ([[Entrepreneurship|Entrepreneurs]]) don't only want to work on the project they borrow for, they also want leisure which decreases the success of the project.
## Collateral
Banks can't measure the commitment or effort of the borrower.

To ensure the entrepreneur has enough commitment, the bank will demand enough assets as collateral such that the borrower has enough *skin in the game*.
## Credit rationing
Firms with too little assets won't get a loan since they can't post enough collateral. 

It is rational for banks to not lend to these firms, even though they would borrow at equilibrium rates. This leads to credit rationing.
# Team formation
In a growing firm, new employees might not have the same incentives as the founding team to be motivated to work hard.

This can be solved by providing employees with an option on company shares that increases in amount of shares over time.
