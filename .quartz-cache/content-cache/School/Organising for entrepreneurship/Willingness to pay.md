Willingness to pay is the amount of money that a customer is willing to pay for a product or service. It is a fundament of [[Value-creation]].
# Determining willingness to pay
Willingness to pay can be determined in several ways:
- Calculate using engineering data (when B2B)
- Regression analysis of historical data
	- [[Structural estimation]] of demand and supply
	- [[Hedonic price equations]]
- Using surveys on the product or service
	- [[Choice analysis]]
	- [[Indifference analysis]]
