[[External intrapreneurial idea generation]] is the practice of firms sourcing external parties for new business ideas. This is useful when two conditions are met, the required innovation:
1. doesn't need depth of expertise.
2. isn't expensive.
# Crowdsourcing
## Models
Four models exists for collaborations with externals for [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] or [[Crowdsourcing]]:
- [[Innovation mall model]]
- [[Innovation community model]]
- [[Elite circle model]]
- [[Consortium]]

These differ across two dimensions:
- **Participation**: open to everyone or closed group?
- **Governance**: hierarchical or flat structure?

## Organizing for crowdsourcing
A firm has two options to practice crowdsourcing:
1. Rely on an [[Innovation intermediary]], such as [[Innocentive]].
2. Develop own [[Crowdsourcing]] platform

# Research
## Effectiveness
Research has shown that externally generated ideas score higher in terms of novelty and customer benefit, but lower on feasibility. This is because externals have it easier with thinking outside of the box, but don't know the internal business so can't assess feasibility.
