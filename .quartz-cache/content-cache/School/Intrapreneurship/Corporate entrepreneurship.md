# Definition
[[Corporate entrepreneurship]] or [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] is a system which allows employees to act like an [[Entrepreneurship|Entrepreneur]] inside an organization. It requires two pivotal things:
1. Entrepreneurial profiles in the company
2. [[Intrapreneurial environment|Entrepreneurial environment]]

In most companies, [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] is a bottom-up process where employees try to convince their management. There are exceptions however, such as Apple which shows that a visionary [[Chief executive officer|CEO]] can create top-down [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]].
# Importance
Firms operate in turbulent environments:
- Technological environment
- Customer environment
- Competitive environment
- Legal and regulatory environment
- Resource environment

These turbulent environments force companies to be dynamic and change, allowing for long-term growth.

[[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] can allow a firm to keep up with these changes and is thus pivotal.

# Ambidextrous firms
Firms should be ambidextrous to be able to efficiently support [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] while achieving long-term performance. They have to practice two important activities:
1. **Exploitation** of existing business, driven by **managers**.
2. **Exploration** of new business, driven by **entrepreneurs**.
## Research
A research that used [[Patent]]s to identify how much a firm invests in existing or new technologies showed that the best performing firms are ambidextrous with an optimal proportion of 80% exploitation - 20% exploration.
## McKinsey Three horizons model
[[#Research|The previous findings]] are supported by the [[McKinsey Three horizons model]]. Good firms practice activities for all three of the horizons. 

In reality most firms focus on horizon one, since this one has the biggest impact on the short-term.

# Required people
In order for [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] to occur, five roles have to be fulfiled:

| Role         | Description                                                                              |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Initiator**    | Triggers [[Corporate entrepreneurship\|Intrapreneurship]] by discovery of an opportunity |
| **Intrapreneur** | Takes lead and drives the project (can be initiator)                                     |
| **Supporter**    | Provides expertise to the project (employees who help)                                   |
| **Sponsor**      | Protects the project and advises                                                         |
| **Mentor**       | Organizationally coaches the intrapreneurs (can be the sponsor)                          |
Organizations have to support these roles by:
- Stimulating idea generation
- Creating incentives for [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]]
- Setting up mentorship programs
# Required character traits
The intrapreneur should possess the following traits:
- Achievement-oriented
- Internal locus of control[^1]
- High tolerance for ambiguity
- Calculated risk-taker
- Autonomous
- Tenacious
# Bootlegging
In order to surface [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] that is subject to [[Bootlegging]], a company has to create incentives for employees to disclose their ideas to the company.

[^1]: Doesn't blame external factors when things go wrong $\leftrightarrow$ external locus of control.
