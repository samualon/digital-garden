A sensing network is a network that a firm sets up, which contains universities and [[Startup|Start-up]]s, to invest in external opportunities. They build this network using:
- University grants
- [[Lablet]]s
- [[Corporate venture capital]]

The network acts as a kind of radar for detecting signals on new opportunities. In this way, the network is valuable as a whole.

[[Incumbent]]s can use this to mitigate disruptions in their industry. It allows them to also hop on the train and evolve.
