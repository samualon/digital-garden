During this phase, firms try to source ideas from their employees and select the best ones. Two general approaches exist for this:
1. [[Assembling an ideation team]]
2. [[Idea challenge]]s

A dedicated ideation team uses a select number of employees, while an [[Idea challenge]] allows many employees to participate. This increases the chances of finding good ideas.
# Research
Research was done on three groups of idea generation methods:
- Voice-of-customer methods
- Open innovation methods: work with partners, vendors, crowdsourcing, etc.
- [[Idea challenge]]

While [[Idea challenge]] were the most used method, their effectiveness is only average. Voice-of customer methods have shown to be more effective.
