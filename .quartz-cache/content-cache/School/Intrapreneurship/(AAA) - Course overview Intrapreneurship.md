# Description
The course aims to teach about practices on [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]], the creation of new businesses in established companies.

It goes over four topics:
1. Importance and nature of intrapreneurship
2. Creating a supportive environment for intrapreneurship
3. Processes and tools
4. Investment in external business opportunities
