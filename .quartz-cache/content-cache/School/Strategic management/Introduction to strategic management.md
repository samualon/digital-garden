A strategy pursues more than profitability.

Strategy can be defined as:
> *The choice of **future** for the organization and a way to reach that future using a framework that coordinates, unifies and **integrates** the company's decisions, actions and **positioning** in an industry to **generate and sustain superior financial returns**.*

It consistently fits all of the following parts together:
- [[Competitive landscape]]
- [[Industry Value System]]
- [[Competitive advantage]]
- [[Scope of a business]]
- [[Activity analysis|Activities]]
- [[Resources and capabilities]]
- [[Business model]]
# Foundations of a strategic decision
![[Foundations of a strategic decision.png]]

A strategic decision is subject to:
- the [[Industry Value System]]
- Firm-specific aspects

It is driven by the vision, mission and values of the firms and requires a concrete implementation.
# Characteristics
A good strategy:
- Is unique
- Uses trade-offs (see [[CR02 What is strategy]])
- Employs **fit** - **coherence** - **consistency**
## Trade-offs
Often, a firm has to make trade-offs between quality offered and costs. A good strategy balances both.
