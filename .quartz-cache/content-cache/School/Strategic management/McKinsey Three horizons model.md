The [[McKinsey]] three horizons model allows companies to think about their future growth. The three horizons each tackle a possible hurdle for future growth.

![[McKinsey Three horizons model.png]]
# Horizon 1
The first horizon focuses on the short-term. It's about clearly defining and reinforcing the core business.

This works as a foundation for later expansion.
# Horizon 2
Horizon 2 works as a bridge between [[#Horizon 1]] and [[#Horizon 3]]. It plans the necessary steps to facilitate an expansion of the business in the long-term.
# Horizon 3
Horizon 3 focuses on the diversification of the business strategy. At this point, the company can exploit new opportunities, start new businesses, etc.
