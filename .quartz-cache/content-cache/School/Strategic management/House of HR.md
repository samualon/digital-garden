Speaker is [[Rika Coppens]] and [[Chief executive officer|CEO]] of House of HR.
# Description
## Origin
Since 2015 expansion outside of Europe.
Network of offices across Europe, based in Kortrijk.

## Activity
Connect and unleash all talent to employers. Investing in career planning and education.
## Structure
10 different *power houses* that are focused on one job specialization/domain. In all these verticals (power houses) are *boutiques* that are even more specialized than the verticals.

HoHR doesn't integrate acquired companies and respect their brand and identity. This makes it easier to retain existing staff in these companies and existing relations with customers (*don't fix what ain't broken*).
## Marketing
Targeted marketing per boutique. This sustains the imago of these different subsidiaries.

No cross-border marketing, but mostly with events, at university...
## Finances
- 3.4B turnover
(see report)
# Strategy
First company to offer [[Temp-to-perm]]. 
## Principles
High focus on 
- Differentiation
- Communication
- Not changing things that work
- Ambition (set high targets)
- Decentralization of accountability and ownership (of *power houses*)
- Allow mistakes (fosters innovation and [[Entrepreneurship]])
- [[Three horizons model]]
- High EBITDA business

## Environment
Crucial question is *Are we in the right market?* Yes, because:
- Aging population
- Need for flexible work
- [[Human resource planning|HRP]] is becoming more important than financial planning
## Ownership
[[Private equity]] ownership. Started with two partners that each had 1/3 and then 1/3 of [[Private equity]].
## Acquisitions
When a *power house* wants to buy a company, the possible acquisition needs to fulfill following criteria:
- Profitable
- Autonomous ability to grow
- Grow faster than the market
- Cultural fit
- Current management stays on board
- KPI culture
### Cultural fit
Alignement between our staff and theirs is needed. *Culture eats strategy for breakfast*. 

### KPI culture
Well-managed companies have good [[Key performance indicator|KPI]]s in place. Furthermore, *What you measure will change*.
## Internal competition
Competition can be good, as long as customers and candidates don't suffer.
## Policy
Not about setting extensive policies, but create a good behavior of employees.

Incentive structure for employees.
