Factors that increase or decrease the cost of an activitiy. They are crucial in [[Building a competitive advantage]] as a company, as they allow to estimate and compare the costs of competitors.
# Categories
Three categories can be made for cost drivers:

| Category                                 | Examples                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Related to firm size                     | [[Economies of scale]], [[Economies of scope]], capacity utilization, ..               |
| Related to cumulative experience         | Learning curve                                                                         |
| Independant (of firm size or experience) | Input prices, location, complexity, government policies, efficiency, agency costs, ... |
