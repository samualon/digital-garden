> "*A firm or a business is said to have created a COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE over its rivals if it has driven a wider wedge between willingness to pay and costs than its competitors have achieved*"
> [[CR08 Strategy and the business landscape|Strategy and the business landscape]]
> 
Competitive advantage refers to a company's ability to outperform its competitors.

A firm with a competitive advantage can't capture more than it's [[Economic value added|Added economic value]] (see [[CR08 Strategy and the business landscape|classic reading]]).
# Types
1. Differentiation competitor
2. Low-cost competitor
3. Dual advantage competitor
# Knowledge
Depends strongly on how a set of codified and [[Tacit knowledge]] is developed, deployed and combined. Policy needs to nurture this process.
