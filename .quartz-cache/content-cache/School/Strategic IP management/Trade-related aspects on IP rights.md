[[Trade-related aspects on IP rights|TRIPS]] is the most comprehensive international agreement on [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]]s, which is required for countries joining the [[World Trade Organization|WTO]].

It deals with minimum guidelines on:
- [[Trade secret]]s
- [[Trademark]]s
- [[Copyright]]s
- [[Patent]]s
- [[Design right]]s
- Geographical indications
- ...
