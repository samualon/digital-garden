R&D has become a global business. Companies develop [[Research and development|R&D]] and file for patents in foreign countries.
# Locating
Research found that companies tend to locate [[Research and development|R&D]] development in countries with:
- High GDP
- Strong IPR protection and enforcement
- Reforms on [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] protection, enforcement, rules, etc.
# IPR protection and domestic firms
Research has shown that the level of [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] protection in a country can have a positive or negative effect on domestic firms, depending on whether its a developed or developing country.
## Developed countries
Domestic firms in **developed countries** benefit from stronger [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]].
## Developing countries
Domestic firms in **developing countries** are hurt by stronger [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]].

This is because firms in developing countries often benefit from imitating other companies, which is prevented by stronger [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]].
# International patent protection
The [[Patent Rights Index]] uses five factors to measure the level of [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] protection in a country. It can be used to compare countries over time.
## World Trade Organization
The [[World Trade Organization]] ([[World Trade Organization|WTO]]) fosters international trade. By lowering importation costs for countries that follow the [[Trade-related aspects on IP rights|TRIPS]], countries globally have improved and converged their [[Patent Rights Index]]es.

## China
China is known for knock-off products. Many companies imitate [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] of other companies. Because China doesn't want to hurt domestic companies, [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] enforcement is low.

However, China offers high-skilled labor, which for foreign companies makes it a difficult trade-off with the risk of [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] infringement.
# Dealing with IPR in weak environments
Five guidelines can be used to practice [[Research and development|R&D]] in countries such as [[#China]] with weak [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] enforcement:
1. [[Internal linkage strategy]]
2. [[Trade secret#By the company|Document and secure your trade secrets]] ([[Internal secrecy policy]])
3. Educate local employees on [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]]
4. Hire local [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] lawyers
5. Develop relations with local government and community
