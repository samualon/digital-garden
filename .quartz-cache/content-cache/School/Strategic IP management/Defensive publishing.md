By publishing an invention to the public, or *state-of-the-art*, it can no longer be [[Patent|Patented]].

Two options exist:
1. Publishing the invention secretly
2. Give visibility to the invention
# Publishing the invention secretly
A company can publish the invention *under the radar*, like Umicor. Umicor published one of their inventions in an African newspaper, making it *state-of-the-art* without making it well-known.
# Giving visibility
A company can publish and disclose their inventions to institutions such as Research disclosure. These institutions have connections with [[Patent]] offices, ensuring that patents can't be registered for the invention.

Registering a [[Patent]] can also be a way to publicize the invention.
