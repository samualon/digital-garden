A trademark is a sign which distinguishes the goods of one enterprise from another.

It lasts for 10 years and is renewable for eternity.
# Conditions
To acquire a trademark, four conditions have to be met. It has to be:
1. A graphic representation
2. Distinctive: no meaning of itself (not connected to the product).
3. Not descriptive
4. Not infringing existing rights
# Classification
Trademarks are only applicable to the classification of products that the company is active in. This means that multiple companies can use similar *trademarks* if their products belong to different classifications.
