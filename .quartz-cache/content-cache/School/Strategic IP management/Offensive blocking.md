Due to the specific nature of patents, competitors might be able to *invent around* your patent. For this reason, a company might attempt to block patenting by competition.

By creating a [[Offensive blocking|Patent fence]], a series of *workarounds* to the invention, it can prevent its competitors from trying to *work-around* and mimic the technology.

- Dupont with Nylon
