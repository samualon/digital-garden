Friction is a measure of how difficult and costly trading of assets is on a market. There are two sources of friction.
## Real sources of friction
- [[Order processing costs]]
- [[Inventory risk]]
- *(Imperfect competition between dealers such as market power)*
## Informational sources of friction
[[Asymmetric information]] will cause dealers to try to compensate for loses due to buying at a price too high or selling at a price too low in case the trader at the opposite end would have better information about $\tilde V$.
