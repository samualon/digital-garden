In a quote driven market, traders don't trade with each other directly, they transact through an intermediary, the dealer, who takes opposite side in all trades.

The dealer supplies liquidity while the traders demand liquidity.
# Organization
[[Quote driven market|Dealer market]]s can vary a lot in practice. In some cases no real-time info might be shown, which requires search costs for traders to find the lowest ask or highest bid prices.
## Brokers and dealers
Brokers and dealers might have deals in place with each other such as:
- [[Preferencing agreement]]
- [[Payment for order flow]]
## Tiers
Typically, [[Quote driven market|Dealer market]]s have a two-tier structure for two different segments:
- Retail segment: for final investors
- Wholesale segment or [[Interdealer market]]: direct trades between dealers to share [[Inventory risk]].

Regularly, this [[Interdealer market]] accounts for 80% of total trade volume, as orders of individual investors go through multiple intermediaries.
