Trading mechanisms:
---
A set of rules used inside the [[Market microstructure]] of financial markets that apply during trading.

Markets are dynamic and so are these rules, they can change over time.
# Classifications
There are many different types of [[Trading infrastructure]]s that we can classify on the basis of three criteria:
- [[Quote driven market]]/[[Quote driven market|DM]] or [[Order driven market]]/[[Order driven market|LOM]]
- [[Call market]] or [[Continuous trading]]
- Transparency

Usually, real life markets are hybrids between these classifications.
## Quote driven vs order driven
| Quote driven (DM)                                       | Order driven (LOM)                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Dealers are present                                     | No dealers, direct interaction between traders                                                   |
| Provide liquidity by taking opposite side of each trade | Traders provide liquidity via [[Limit order]]s, but also demand liquidity with [[Market order]]s |
| Guaranteed liquidity (big banks oppose trades)          | No guarantee for liquidity (no big banks necessarily oppose trades)                              |
## Transparency
The degree of transparency can also function as a categorization measure. Both pre- and post-trade metrics can increase or decrease transparency.
### Pre-trade
- Quotes (bid and ask)
- Depth
- All prices are shown or only best prices
- Identify of traders
### Post-trade
- Size of orders executed
- Direction of orders executed (sells or buys)
- Identify of traders
