# Introduction
Most financial courses talk about portfolio creation, risk management, buy/sell recommendations, etc.

This course focuses on the systems that are in place on the trading and exchanges markets, f.e. price formation and other pricing mechanisms.

A simple stock trade makes multiple problems arise:
- Venue of trade?
- Types of orders in venues?
- Origin of set prices?
- Strategy to employ to counter:
	- [[Asymmetric information]]
	- Faster traders
	- Risk of buyer/seller not delivering

To understand these problems, we need knowledge of:
- [[Market microstructure]]
- [[Trading infrastructure]]

Trading and exchanges will focus on some recurring themes:
- [[Price formation]]
- [[Market liquidity]]
- [[Price discovery]]
- [[Volatility]]
- [[Market stability]]
- [[Welfare]]
