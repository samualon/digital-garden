A firm is financially constraint when it has a need for external financing, but isn't able to obtain it. 

This can be seen as a friction for firms to fund all desired investment opportunities.
# Supply and demand
![[Supply and demand of external financing.png]]
If demands are higher than supply, banks will increase their intrest rates.

If demands are lower than supply, banks will decrease their intrest rates.

Some firms might not be able to attract funds, even at this equilibrium rate, because of [[Credit rationing]].
# Implications
Financial constraints impact:
- Export
- Investments
- Asset growth
- Sales growth
- Employment growth
- Startup formation
