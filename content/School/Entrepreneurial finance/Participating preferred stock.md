[[Participating preferred stock]] gives, like a [[Convertible stock]], an investor a fixed return with a preferred term. Additionally, when the firm exits, the investor can receive their share of the exit value:
$$ CF_{Inv} = PT + (X - PT) * F_{Inv} $$
With:
- $CF_{Inv}=$ Cash flow to the investor
- $X =$ Exit value of the firm
- $PT=$ [[Preferred term]] of the investor
- $F_{Inv}=$ Ownership share of the investor

# Capped
To preserve incentives for the entrepreneur, a cap might be put on the share of the investor when an exit occurs:
$$ X>X^{CAP} \Rightarrow Participation \: vanishes $$
$$ \Downarrow $$
$$ CF_{Inv} = PT + (X-PT) * F_{Inv} $$
