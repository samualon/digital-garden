The FUEL framework is a framework for understanding [[Entrepreneurial finance]].

This reflects the investor's nature and approach.
# Components
The [[FUEL framework]] consists of four major components:
1. [[#Fundamental structure]]
2. [[#Underlying motivation]]
3. [[#Expertise and networks]]
4. [[#Logic and style]]

Each components correlates to a question:

| Component                | Question                     |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| *Fundamental structure*  | Who is the investor?         |
| *Underlying motivation*  | What does he/she want?       |
| *Expertise and networks* | What does he/she contribute? |
| *Logic and style*        | How does he/she operate?     |
## Fundamental structure
This describes the identity of the investor and entails:
- Investor's organizational structure and financial resources
- Decision-making structure (governance)

## Underlying motivation
This describes the demands of the investor and entails:
- The valuation used by the investor for (non-) financial returns
- Their risk-tolerance and patience
## Expertise and networks
This describes the contribution of the investor and entails:
- Expertise and network of the investor
- Investor's standing with their peers
## Logic and style
This describes how the investor operates and entails:
- **Logic**: How does the investor select companies?
- **Style**: How does the investor interact with these companies?
