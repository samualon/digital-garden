Vesting is the practice of letting founders of a firm *earn back* their stocks through two criteria:
- Time vesting (get stock back after a certain time has passed)
- Performance vesting (get stock back when reaching f.e. IPO)
# Cliff
A cliff can be defined, which determines the starting point from where stocks can be earned back.

F.e. "Quarterly vesting of 6% with a cliff of one year" would imply that after two years the founder would have received 24% of his stocks ($Y1 = 0 (cliff); \: Y2 = 4 * 6\%; \: Total = Y1 +Y2 = 24\%$).
