Before investing (or starting a business), the opportunity should be evaluated. It answers the important question:
> Will the opportunity be profitable enough (for a certain risk)?
*(for the entrepreneur AND the investors)*

A well known tool is the [[Venture Evaluation Matrix]].
# Motivation
Evaluating a business opportunity is not only important for investors, also for the entrepreneurs themselves. It gives them the perspective of the investors and can help answer questions like:
- Will the venture generate enough profits for investors?
- How can the venture be refined and improved?
