The [[Internal rate of return|IRR]] is the discount rate that would create an [[Net Present Value|NPV]] of $0$.
$$ NPV = \sum^N_{n=0} \frac{C_n}{(1+IRR)^n} = 0 $$
# Pro and con
| Pro                                        | Con                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Takes [[Time value of money]] into account | Disregards risk carried by the investment |
