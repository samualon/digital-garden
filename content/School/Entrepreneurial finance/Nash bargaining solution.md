The [[Nash bargaining solution]] offers a way to theorize and simplify negotiations on deals. This can be used during [[Investment deal structuring]] in an [[Entrepreneurship]] context.

The theory converges negotiations to three key variables that correspond to three concepts:

| Variable                                                                                    | Concept                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Outside option: the value a party gets when choosing to walk away from the deal             | Each party's outside option          |
| Joing value: the value that the two parties achieve when cooperating                        | Zone Of Possible Arrangements (ZOPA) |
| Splitting the surplus: the division of the surplus between making the deal and walking away | Final agreement                      |

# Outside option
A party gets a certain value when walking away from the deal (f.e. choosing another investment for investors and choosing another investor for the entrepreneur). It gives parties power to walk away so they're crucial.
# ZOPA
The ZOPA maps the zone of good possible deals that for each party is are more attractive than the outside option.
# Example
This example shows a negotiation between an investor and an entrepreneur in function of the downside protection of the investor and the valuation of the firm.

![[Nash bargaining example.png]]

The entrepreneur is willing to increase downside protection if the valuation of the firm increases too (more downside protection for a higher price per share).

The investor doesn't want a deal under a certain amount of downside protection.

Which deal eventually gets chosen in the ZOPA depends on deal-specific conditions (f.e. negotiation ability).
## Formulae
$$ SV = JV - OO $$
$$ BV_E = OO_E + BS_E * SV $$
$$ BV_I = OO_I + BS_I * SV $$

With:

| Notation | Meaning                    |
| -------- | -------------------------- |
| $SV$     | Surplus                    |
| $JV$     | Joint value of cooperation |
| $OO$     | Outside option             |
| $BV$     | Bargaining value           |
| $BS$     | Bargaining strength        |
| $E$      | Entrepreneur               |
| $I$      | Investor                   |
