# Summary
Some firms have [[Financial constraints]], which prevents them to invest in certain crucial parts of their business.

These [[Financial constraints]] arise due to [[Credit rationing]], where they're unable to attract funding at or above equilibrium intrest rates from banks. 

Credit rationing often occurs because of two consequences of [[Asymmetric information]]:
1. [[Adverse selection]]
2. [[Moral hazard]]
