
This approach of determining [[Financial projections#Estimating revenues|estimate revenues]], uses supply-side logic and is supported by [[Primary data]] (company data).

This approach starts from the company and how far it can scale its units. It uses customer's [[Willingness to pay]] for the product specific to the venture.
# Pro and con
| Pro                                      | Con                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Works well for very large or new markets | Requires estimating realistic growth rate of production capacity |
|                                          | Need for realism (returns, discounts, etc.)                      |
