Exit, a [[Liquidity event]], offers [[Shareholders]] a way to receive returns on their equity investment. Since founders usually don't get high salaries, this event is important to them.

An exit can occur in the downside, when the firm needs to choose between continuing or closing, and in the upside.
# Motivation
- Liquidity
- Need for new owners
- Market timing (favorabel conditions)
# Types
There are three major types of exit:
1. [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]]
2. [[Acquisition]]
3. [[Financial sale as exit|Financial sale]]
