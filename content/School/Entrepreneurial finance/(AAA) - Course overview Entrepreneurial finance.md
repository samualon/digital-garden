# Description
## Content
This course by [[Mike Mariathasan]] aims to adres financing and strategic challenges faced by [[Entrepreneurship|Entrepreneurs]] in the early stage of firm creation. It discusses the possible sources of finance and typical valuation methods.

Some more specific topics are:
- Valuation methods for private firms
- Economics of contracts
- [[Exit strategy]]
## Assessment
Grading is done with a final exam. A one-sided (printed or written) A4 cheat sheet is allowed. Your name should be printed on the top of the page in Times New Roman size 20.

A non-programmable calculator is also allowed.
