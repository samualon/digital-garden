The FIRE, **F**it **I**nvest **R**ide **E**xit, framework is a framework for understanding [[Entrepreneurial finance]].
# Stages
Four stages:
1. [[#Fit]]
2. [[#Invest]]
3. [[#Ride]]
4. [[#Exit]]
## Fit
The first step is to find a fit between entrepreneur and investor.

This brings two big challenges:
1. **Search challenge**: how do we find relevant partners?
2. **Selection challenge**: how do we screen potential partners (due diligence)?
## Invest
This step is about closing the deal between entrepreneur and investor, money for ownership.

Four forces affect this deal:
1. Needs of the entrepreneur
2. Needs of the investor (see [[FUEL framework]])
3. Expectations about the venture's future
4. Market conditions
## Ride
This encompasses the cooperative process of the growing the firm. It requires two things:
- Learning about the company, market and each other
- Setting up governance (who decides what?)

Often, [[Staged financing]] goes along this growth.
## Exit
In this stage, investors sell some or all of their shares and finally realize a return.

Two [[Exit decisions]] are included in this stage:
1. When to exit
2. How to exit
