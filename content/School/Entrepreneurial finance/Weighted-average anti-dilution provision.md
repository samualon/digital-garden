An [[Anti-dilution mechanism]] where the ownership of shares of an investor of an early round is recalculated using a weighted average.

The first round is re-priced at:
$$ P^{WA} = P_1 * \frac{\frac{S_{BASE}+ I}{P_1}}{\frac{S_{BASE}+ I}{P_2}}$$
With: 
- $P_x=$ share price at round $x$
- $I=$ shares invested by investor
- $S_{BASE}=$ either
	- narrow based = [[Serie A]]
	- broad based = including [[Entrepreneurship|Entrepreneur]]s shares
