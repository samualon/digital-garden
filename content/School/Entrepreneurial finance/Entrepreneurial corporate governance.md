[[Corporate Governance]] is a set of actions that allow an investor to ensure receiving a return.
# Motivation
There are two motivators for [[Corporate Governance]]:
1. Companies need guidance
2. Investors need protection of returns
## Companies need guidance
- Entrepreneurial drive doesn't come from experience
- Young [[Startup|Start-up]]s need mentorship and access to networks
## Investors need protection of returns
Companies need mechanisms in place to increase their returns while reducing the risk of losing money.
# Control structures
Besides the control implied by the company's charter and legal documents (bylaws, shareholder agreement, etc.), there are three control structures for investors:
1. Voting rights
2. [[Board of Directors]]
3. Informal control
## Voting rights
Voting rights allow [[Shareholders]] to make decisions. By requiring shares with more voting power (f.e. two votes per share for investor's shares), they can increase their power to make decisions.
## Board of directors
The [[Board of Directors]] makes key strategic decisions:
- Product
- Market
- Hiring and firing of executives
- Alliances
- Fundraising

The board carries the duty to act in the intrest of the [[Shareholders]] first.
### Roles
The [[Board of Directors]] has four roles:
1. **Boss**: decision-making
2. **Monitor**: oversee the company on behalf of the [[Shareholders]]
3. **Coach**: advice the [[Chief executive officer|CEO]]
4. **Promotor**: forge new relationships
### Composition
A [[Board of Directors]] is composed of three categories of people:
1. [[Entrepreneurship|Entrepreneur]]s and managers
2. Investors
3. Independent directors
## Informal control
Additional informal factors can impact the decisions made:
- **[[Power of the purse]]**: investors can influence decisions by the additional money that they could provide for the company.
- **Power of personality**: getting trust and respect 
- **Power of persuasion**: experience and argumentation
# Value addition
Investors can add value using four activities:

| Activity                   | Relevant level | Description                                                            |
| -------------------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mentoring and coaching** | Personal level | Helping entrepreneurs cope with the challenges of [[Entrepreneurship]] |
| **Advising**               | Company levell | Support strategic decision-making                                      |
| **Networking**             | Industry level | Creating connections with other players in the industry                |
| **Pressuring**             | Company level  | Push management to think about strategy                                |
