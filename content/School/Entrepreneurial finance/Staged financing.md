Funding to a project is done over several rounds, a milestone-based approach.

This has benefits and costs for the investors as well as the entrepreneurs:

|          | Entrepreneur benefits     | Investor benefits                                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Benefits | Reduces fundraising costs | Creates *option value* of waiting<br><br>Ability to increase control through money |
| Costs    | [[Refinancing risk]]      |                                                                                    |
# Terminology
[[Funding rounds]] are called *Series* followed by an alphabetical letter. 

Pre-VC rounds are called seed.
