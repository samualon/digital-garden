[[Anti-dilution mechanism]] protects early investors from dilution of their ownership when new shares are issued. This is especially relevant during a [[Down round]] when the share price of the new share is lower than the price that the investor originally paid.

There's an analogy with [[Convertible stock]]. [[Convertible stock]] protects investors from disappointing exits. [[Anti-dilution mechanism]] protects the investor from disappointing funding rounds.
# Types
Two main methods exist:
- [[Full-ratchet anti-dilution provision]]
- [[Weighted-average anti-dilution provision]]

In both cases, the amount of shares owned by the investor is recalculated with the new anti-dilution price.
