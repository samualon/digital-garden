[[Investment syndication]] is the practice of investors finding other investors to provide funding for one company.
# Advantages
This has several advantages:
- Obtain a second opinion
- Reduce commitment into the company (allows for more diversification)
- Offer the investor complementary skills and networks
# Structure
Syndications usually have two parties:
- Lead investor: performs [[Due diligence]], negotiates, sits on [[Board of Directors]].
- Followers: provide capital and expertise + networks
# Performance impact
Studies show that syndicated deals tend to perform better.
