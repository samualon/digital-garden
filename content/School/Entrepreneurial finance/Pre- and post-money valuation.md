Pre-money valuation is the valuation of the business before an investment is made.

Post-money valuation is the valuation of the business after an investment is made.

As:
$$ Pre \: money \: value = Post \: money \: value - Investment $$
Which means:
$$ Post \: money \: value = Pre \: money \: value - Investment $$
Or:
$$ V_{post} = V_{pre} - I $$
