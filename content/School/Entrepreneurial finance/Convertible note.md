[[Convertible note]]s are debt-like claims for investors that:
- are automatically converted into stock on [[Serie A]] investment.
- offer a discount on the conversion (more stock for a smaller price).
- mature on average after 1 - 2 years.
# Advantages
A [[Convertible note]] is appealing for [[Startup|Start-up]]s because:
- Simple and standard structure (little legal costs)
- Gives no control or protective rights away
- Postpone valuation and negotiation to the [[Serie A]] round
# Formulae
## Conversion rate
$$ P_{CN} = (1-DC) * P_{INV} $$
With $P =$ share price and $DC=$ discount rate.
## Ownership
$$ S_{CN} = \frac{I_{CN}}{P_{CN}} = \frac{I_{CN}}{(1-DC) * P_{INV}}$$
With $I=$ investment amount and $S_{CN} =$ number of [[Convertible note]] shares.
## Valuation cap
Since the typical 10 - 20% discount can be very expensive in case of success, a valuation cap can be introduced:
$$ P_{CN} = \frac{V^{CAP}}{S_{PRE}} \leq (1-DC) * P_{INV}$$
$$ P_{INV} = \frac{V_{PRE}}{S_{PRE}+S_{CN}}$$
