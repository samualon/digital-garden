The practice of setting up terms in a contract that are conditional, such as reaching milestones.

As [[Entrepreneurship]] is a highly uncertain context, contracts are made *incomplete*. Through [[Contingent contracting]], the parties can set conditional clauses.
# Milestones
Milestones can be used as conditions in the [[Term sheets|Term sheet]], these can be:
- Financial
- Operational
- Managerial
- Technical

Additional funding is often conditional to these milestones.

Milestones have drawbacks:
- Entrepreneur quickly tries to put prototype together because of the time constraint.
- Bringing a product to the market to early.
- Short-term vision
