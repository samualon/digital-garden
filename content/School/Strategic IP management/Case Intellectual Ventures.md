Intellectual Ventures is a very big [[Non-practicing IP entity]]. Founder is Nathan Myhrvold.
# Creating the portfolio
IV built a big patent portfolio by:
- Acquiring patents
- Acquiring pre-filing inventions
- Inventing using their engineers

About 35.000 patents focused on information, technology and IT patents.

Having a big portfolio offers an advantage to them over smaller [[Non-practicing IP entity]]. It's more difficult for companies to work around IV patents

Disadvantages:
- Need for lots of capital
- Risky (valuable or not valuable patents?)
# Revenues
They receive revenue streams through licencing, but difficult because:
- Deals take a lot of time
- Deals need experts
# Is IV a patent troll?
No:
- They also develop technologies themselves, not only acquisitions.
- Doesn't wait until irreversible infringement of firms on their patents.
- Not focusing on vulnerable company status

Who likes IV?
- Big players, as IV reduces the search costs for these big companies.
- Smaller companies because they often don't have the money to get patents and fully develop.
