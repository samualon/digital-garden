Companies can use patents strategically and employ patent blocking.

Two forms of patent blocking exist:
- [[Offensive blocking]] or a [[Offensive blocking|Patent fence]]
- [[Defensive blocking]]
# Measuring
Using the citations to a companies own patents or the patents of a competitor, it is possible to measure whether a company practices [[Offensive blocking]] and [[Defensive blocking]].

A company which has many self-citations (own patents reference own patents) shows [[Offensive blocking]].

A company which has many citations to a competitor's patents shows [[Defensive blocking]].
# Research
Research has shown that [[Defensive blocking]] has a positive correlation with [[Cross-licensing]], which is also positively correlated to firm performance.
