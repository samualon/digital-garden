A trade secret is any confidential information of a business that gives it an economic benefit and thus a [[Competitive advantage]]. This advantage is directly derived from the fact that the information is unknown to competitors as the company tries to keep it secret. 
# Conditions
Business information is a trade secret in case that:
1. It offers a [[Competitive advantage]].
2. It's unknown to competitors.
3. The owner does efforts to keep it secret.
# Protection
Both the state and the company introduce ways to protect trade secrets.
## By the state
The trade secret EU directive 2016/943 gives a definition and ensures protection of the trade secret. 

It protects the trade secret from **illegal acces**:
- Unauthorized access to documents, objects, etc.
- Breaching of [[Non-disclosure agreements|NDA]]s and [[Non-compete agreement|NCA]]s

It doesn't protect against **legal access**:
- Independent discovery or creation
- Reverse engineering
## By the company
- Control of publishing by researchers and employees
- Control access to facilities
- Monitoring visitors
- Avoiding [[Patent|Patenting]]
- Implementing an [[Internal secrecy policy]]
- Increase loyalty in employees
- Prevent hiring of key personnel by competitors
- Fragmenting information over multiple managers and employees
