A grant-back clause allows a licensor of an [[IP licensing]] to receive the improvements that a licensee creates. This way, the licenser can benefit from the improvements that the licensee develops.

They receive the right to license the new technology of the licensee.
# Research
Research has shown the following:
1. Grant-back clauses are more probable when the technology is more core of the licensor.
2. Grant-back clauses are less probable when the technology is more core of the licensee.
