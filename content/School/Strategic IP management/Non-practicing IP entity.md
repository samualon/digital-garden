A [[Non-practicing IP entity]], or [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]], is a company that acquires [[IP]] for the sole purpose of extracting payments from infringers that didn't do enough research and didn't see that the technology was patented by the troll.

These [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent shark]]s act opportunistically. They wait until the soon-to-be infringer has committed irreversible investments before making claims on infringements.

# Research
[[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]]s are increasing in popularity. Research is thus relevant.
## Victims
Research has found that they target:
- cash rich firms
- profitable firms
- firms with a small legal team
- firms that currently have other lawsuits

Additionally, [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent shark]]s will choose courts to file the lawsuit in states that have a strong stance against patent infringement, such as Waco in Texas.
## Impact on innovation
Research has shown that firms that have lost against a [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]] will reduce [[Research and development|R&D]] spending. [[Non-practicing IP entity]]s thus have a negative impact on innovation.
# Protection
[[Defensive patent aggregator]]s can be used by firms to protect against such a [[Non-practicing IP entity]].
