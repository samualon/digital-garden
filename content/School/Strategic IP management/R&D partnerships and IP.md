R&D partnership can speed up development, as a smaller company can share resources to develop a technology.

However, failure rate is between 20 and 40%. Cooperation can fail because of:
- Disagreements in [[Research and development|R&D]]
- Disagreements in [[Intellectual property rights]]
# Reducing failure
Failure in cooperation can be preventen by doing the following:
1. Select the right partners
2. Coordinate using good contracts
