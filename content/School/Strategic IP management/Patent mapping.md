[[Patent mapping]] is the practice of looking for patents in a certain technological field and generating analysis reports.
# Examples
## Patent activity per firm over time
By tracking which companies in a field acquire patents at what time, firms can determine which companies are investing or divesting in a technology. Firms with decreasing patents on the technology over time might be willing to sell their patents.
