[[IP life cycle management]] offers a way to strategically manage R&D efforts of [[Intellectual property rights|IP]].
# Phases
[[IP life cycle management]] proposes three phases of R&D efforts to performance and market adoptation:
1. Develop and protect the technology
2. Promote the technology
3. Harvest the technology

![[IP life cycle management.png]]
## Develop and protect the technology
In the first stage, companies should:
- Protect [[Intellectual property rights|IP]]
	- Apply for [[Patent]]s, [[Design right]]s, [[Trademark]]s
	- Apply [[Trade secret]]s
	- [[Defensive publishing]]
- Create [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] portfolios
	- [[Offensive blocking]] (in discrete product industries)
	- [[Defensive blocking]] (in complex product industries)
- Develop collaborative technology
	- [[Background IP]]
	- [[Foreground IP]]
## Promote the technology
In the second stage, companies should:
- Focus on creating a standard from your product ($\uparrow$ adoptation)
- [[Open IP strategy]]
	- License IP widely to business partners
	- Put IP in [[Public domain]]
	- Pool IP in [[Patent pool]]s
## Harvest the technology
In the last phase, companies should:
- [[Value-capturing]] from the technology (which is now standardized)
- Enforce against [[Patent#Infringement|IP infringement]]
- Seek applications of the IP in other industries/geographies
