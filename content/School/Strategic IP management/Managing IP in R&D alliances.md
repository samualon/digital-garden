# Equity or contracts
Whether two companies should share equity (joint ownership) or use a one-off deal with contracts introduces a trade-off between costs of establishing a joint-venture and control. Joint-ventures cost more but offer more control, one-offs cost less but offer less control.
# Co-location and separate research
Co-location improves cooperation of the project but risks [[Spillovers]] of valuable information to the other firm.
