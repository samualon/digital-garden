Taiwanese company [[Foxconn]], an incumbent in their industry. Then [[BYD]] had a lot of technological upgrades and became a competitor of Foxconn.
# Preparation questions
1. What are the strategies used by BYD to leverage the IP of its competitors? 
2. What management practices at Foxconn have facilitated the success of BYD’s learning-by-hiring strategy? 
3. What are the loopholes in Foxconn’s IT systems? 
4. Should Foxconn pursue criminal action against BYD?
# Answers
## Used strategies by BYD
1. Learning by hiring (illegal bcs of NDAs)
2. Get access to public documents
3. Reverse engineering
### Learning-by-hiring
BYD set up recruitment offices specifically to poach workers from Foxconn. This way they could incorporate the knowledge of these workers in their own company.

This was illegal because of the [[Non-disclosure agreements|NDA]]s that these employees had signed. The employees' act is illegal, but for [[BYD]] only if it was orchastrated (which was the case).
### Public documents
They used a large number of non-patented technologies and combined them with their own knowledge.
## Management practices supporting succes of BYD's learning-by-hiring
Chinese and Taiwanese employees in [[Foxconn]] were treated inequal. Taiwanese workers were treated better (often white collar workers):
- No unions
- Stringent cost control for blue collar workers
- Career prospects
- Salary

BYD used these bad working conditions to attract Foxconn workers, by offering a better environment

Furthermore, there were big loopholes in Foxconn's IT security and there was a too wide access to trade secrets.
## Decision to pursue criminal action against BYD
| Pro | Contra |
| ---- | ---- |
| Sends a strong signal to other companies that you're serious about IP. | May get some bad reputation as an 'agressive' company. |
| There is supportive evidence (dedicated recruitment office, seized Foxconn documents on BYD hard-disks, got successful very fast without investing lots in R&D) | In the process of proving this, the trade secrets may get more disclosed to the public. |
|  | BYD has experience in these types of cases. |
|  | Legal fees |
|  | Hard to proof orchestration. |
|  | Priority should be on prevention and not on revenge. |
