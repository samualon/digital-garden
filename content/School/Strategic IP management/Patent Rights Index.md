The [[Patent Rights Index]] is a measure of [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] protection in a country. It uses five metrics:
1. **Coverage**: what is patentable?
2. **Membership in international treaties**: has the country signed international [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] agreements with other countries.
3. **Duration of protection**
4. **Enforcement mechanisms**: f.e. judges can injuct (oordelen) before trials, speeding up handling.
5. **Restrictions on patent rights**: has a negative effect on score, are there strict requirements?
