An [[Innovation ecosystem]] is a network of organizations who collaborate to create new innovations ([[Intellectual property rights|IP]]).
# Types
Two types of innovation ecosystems exist:
- [[Consortium-based innovation ecosystem]]
- [[Orchestrator-based innovation ecosystem]]
# IMEC
[[IMEC]] is the orchestrator in an [[Innovation ecosystem]].
## Industrial Affiliation Programs (IAP)
[[IMEC]] creates IAP applied research paths, where they develop research programs and invite companies to collaborate. [[IMEC]] then functions as an [[Orchestrator-based innovation ecosystem|orchestrator]].

The project is divised into groups, where companies work on different parts of the development.

## IP model
[[IMEC]] already owns some [[Background IP]]. All companies that join an IAP can use this [[Intellectual property rights|Intellectual property]].

By collaborating, companies jointly create [[Foreground IP]]. Three types of rights for this IP exist:
1. R0
2. R1
3. R2
### R0
When [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] is created by an [[IMEC]] employee, only [[IMEC]] has rights to it.
### R1
Most [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] of [[IMEC]] falls under this form.

When a company co-develops IP with IMEC, both IMEC and the company hold rights. Other companies in the IAP however, can get licenses.
### R2
When a company co-develops IP with [[IMEC]], but the IP is only relevant for the company, then only the company becomes owner of the IP.
# Dual core-dual site orchestrator model
In this model, two orchestrators work on one innovation, but for two different ecosystems. Similar [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] rules apply.
