Apple is a popular target for [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]]s because:
- Big profitable company
- High-tech / [[Complex industry]]
# Changing legislation
Apple wants to change this trend, lobbying for changes in patent legislation, because:
- Many low quality patents
	- 95% in the US get accepted for patent
	- Much less research during patent applications
- Claims in patents are too vague
- [[Forum shopping]]
- Preliminary injunction[^1]
## Possible solutions
- Abolish preliminary injections
- Court costs aren't split but are for the litigator ([[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]])
- Court that is cheaper and faster for invalidating patents (easier to invalidate patents of the [[Non-practicing IP entity|Patent troll]])
- Focus on invalidating the patents of the troll
- Create stronger patent portflios ([[Offensive blocking]])
- [[Defensive patent aggregator]]
