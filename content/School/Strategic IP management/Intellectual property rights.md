[[Intellectual property rights]] provides exclusive rights to an inventor's or company's intellectual property.
# Motivation
[[Innovation]] is a valuable change/improvement. There's a need for innovation since it increases:
- Welfare
- Living standards

[[Innovation]] is a challenging task:

![[Innovation#Challenges]]

We need [[Intellectual property rights|IP]] to stimulate and foster innovation, to make it attractive regardless of the challenges.
# Types
Four types of intellectual property exist:
- [[Patent]]
- [[Design right]]
- [[Copyright]]
- [[Trademark]]
## Comparison
|              | [[Patent]]               | [[Design right]]                | [[Copyright]]               | [[Trademark]]                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| *Use*        | New technical inventions | Outward appearance of a product | Original and creative works | Distinctive identification of products or services |
| *Acquireing* | Registration             | Automatic or registration       | Automatic                   | Registration                                       |
| *Protection* | 20 years                 | 3 - 25 years                    | Life of creator + 70 years  | 10 years, $\infty$ renewals                        |
| *Example*    | Windmill technology      | Soda can appearance             | Software, songs             | Google                                             |
