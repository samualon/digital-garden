An [[Internal linkage strategy]] is an [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] strategy where a company segments its [[Research and development|R&D]] projects into subprojects and lets some of those subproject be executed in companies that have weak [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]] but other advantages.

This way, the company can control which sensitive IP gets put in which country. It does however come with:
- Coordination costs
- Integration costs
# Research
Research has found that companies increase the use of [[Internal linkage strategy]] when:
- developing in countries with weak [[Intellectual property rights|IPR]]
- the technology is codifiable[^1]
- the technology isn't complex (discrete technology)

[^1]: Technology that's not [[Tacit knowledge|tacit]] and thus easy to copy and pass on.
