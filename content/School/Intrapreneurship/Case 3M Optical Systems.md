One of the most intrapreneurial organizations. Uses the [[Intrapreneurial environment#Organizational ownership and resources|Enabler model]]. 15% of free time is for *pet projects*.

Very decentralized organization, many [[Strategic Business Unit|SBU]]s with own p&l statements. One of these units was Optical Systems managed by Andy Wong. He had to make an important decission. His team asked for more funding for the development of a new multiprotection filter (protected computer + privacy viewing). Andy had then to ask for the money from his manager.

| Pro                                                         | Contra                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unique product                                              | Puts own credibility at stake                                                   |
| Have learned from failures (already tried similar products) | [[Red ocean strategy\|Red ocean]] product industry, four other big competitors. |
| His unit was loss-making and needed *home run* product.     | Too optimistic sales forecast (estimated extravagant market share)              |

Andy Wong was operating level manager (lowest rank of managers). Roles:
- Focus on profitability of his unit (short-term), by:
	- cost-cutting
	- rationalizing the portfolio (some products that were loss-making)
- Identify growth opportunities
- Build a good team
- Motivate his team
- Get funding for the projects

Paul Geller was Vice president of the division of Optical Systems. He had to choose if he wanted to approve the project and funding.

| Pro                                                                           | Contra                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Support growth opportunities                                                  | He can give the money to another [[Strategic Business Unit\|SBU]] |
| Not much money (750k USD)                                                     | OS has been loss-making                                           |
| Rigurous project review was done for the new product (so viable and feasible) | Corporate pressure to close down the OS unit                      |
|                                                                               |                                                                   |

At the end he approved the request. It was a home run and made the whole division profitable.

Role of the senior level manager such as Paul Geller:
- Support [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]]/[[Entrepreneurship]] (by supporting projects)
- Monitor profitability of [[Strategic Business Unit|SBU]]s
- Discipline [[Strategic Business Unit|SBU]]s to get profitable

How is 3M such an intrapreneurial organization. 
- ambitions is 30% of sales come from products that are less than 4y old.
- entrepreneurial environment:
	- 15% rule
	- policy of borrowing technology (products are of divisions, but technologies are of the whole company)
	- Tolerant to well-intended failures
