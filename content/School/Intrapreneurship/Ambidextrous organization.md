An [[Ambidextrous organization]] is an organization with two separate units, one for exploitation (current business) and one for exploration (new business opportunities).

These two pursue different goals, the exploitation focuses on returns while the exploration focuses on growth.

However, interaction is needed between the two in order to share knowledge. This can be done by implementing a good mission and vision statement and common-team rewards (rewards based on performance of both businesses).

A second way of accomplishing this interaction is through [[Spacial ambidexterity]].
# Research
Research shows:
- [[Ambidextrous organization]] is a common choice (15 out 34).
- Firms that become ambidextrous increase in performance of:
	- New exploring business
	- Existing exploiting business
