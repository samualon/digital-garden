Airline companies face many challenges today:
- Price sensitive customers
- Risky industry
- Sustainability regulations due to pollution and noise levels
- Fast-growing industry, need new technologies to be able to accommodate

KLM has two approaches to [[Corporate venturing unit|Corporate venturing]]:
- KLM has their own [[Corporate venturing unit]]
- [[Corporate venturing unit]] Mainport Innovation Fund with TU Delft, Rabobank and Schiphol.

MIF focuses on aviation innovation. Participating in this fund has advantages and disadvantages for KLM:

| Advantages                                                                                                             | Disadvantages                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spread risk through funding of other parties for development (8 million in total, 1mil per party and 4mil of NL state) | Difficult to make consensuses, needs joint decision-making |
| Access to more industry (KLM and Schiphol), technical (TU Delft) and financial (Rabobank) knowledge                    | Government imposes only Dutch [[Startup\|Start-up]]s       |
|                                                                                                                        | 8 million is small fund (= 6 to 10 investments possible)   |
The investment committee (mostly CFOs) make the investment decissions.
