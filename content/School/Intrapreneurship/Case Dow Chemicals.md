New business is created: an online sales channel for the epoxy business of Dow (internet business boom).

Bottom-up: intrapreneur was sales director (middle management), Ian Telford (wasn't Bootlegged).

Ian believed that e-commerce for Dow's epoxy was an opportunity because:
- Idle production capacity
- Mostly focuses on big customers (could target smaller clients)

Asked leader of his division for funding. Reasons pro and contra:

| Pro                             | Contra                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Growth opportunity              | Have little experience with e-commerce                                                                                                                              |
| Can be first mover              | Transparent pricing (since pricing of epoxy would be listed on the site, no longer option for individual prices with big customers), issue of sales canibalisation. |
| Support passionate intrapreneur | Already similar initiatives that are being done at Dow                                                                                                              |
| It's not much money             |                                                                                                                                                                     |

But he got the funding and implemented some changes to mitigate the contra:
- Sales cannibalization $\rightarrow$ He set the price higher than the price of key accounts 

He created fake news that competitor was making a similar project to convince epoxy managers to give more funding (million USD), but it was obviously fake so it was okay.

Successful because:
- Low-cost
- First mover
- Ian is passionate intrapreneur
- Built a good team
- (Sales) managers believed in him and protected

Ian Telford is the key of the succes, he has the traits of a good intrapreneur:
- Forward-looking (visionary)
- Risk-taker
- Motivating (convincing)
- Creative
- Lobbying for resources (money and help from collogues)
- Self-confident

An intrapreneur isn't constrained by financial resources, but needs to be good at lobbying for these resources.

The sponsors, the managers, who support should support by:
- Offering financial resources
- Give feedback (mentor)
- Promoting the idea to higher management
- Protect the idea in early stages
