A [[Corporate venturing unit]] is a unit that makes small investments into promising [[Startup|Start-up]]s.

The corporation gains a board seat from this, which allows them to receive much information from the [[Startup|Start-up]].

The [[Startup|Start-up]] gains mentorship.

Two corporations can set this up jointly using a [[Joint venturing fund]].
# Popularity
When disruptive innovations emerge, corporate venturing increases (f.e. the internet).
# Success factors
There are five success factors for a [[Corporate venturing unit]]:
1. Goals
2. Long-term commitment
3. Investment
4. Autonomy
5. Adjacency
## Goal clarity and focus
The goals of the venturing should be clearly defined: 
- Financial returns?
- Idea generation?
- Strategic long-term reasons?

The goal of the venturing decides who the sponsor of the unit should be (CEO? CFO? CTO? ...)
## Long-term commitment
Learning from a [[Corporate venturing unit]] takes a long time, so management should be patient and not dismantle it too quickly.
## Critical mass of investment
Enough funding should be provided to the [[Corporate venturing unit]].
## Autonomy
The corporation shouldn't limit the autonomy of the [[Corporate venturing unit]] to choose investments, as corporations are usually risk-averse and thus won't invest in possibly innovative projects.

The [[Corporate venturing unit]] should have autonomy in the investments.
## Adjacency
Companies shouldn't try to grow in completely different industries than their own, but should stick to *adjacent* industries, technologies, etc (f.e. Samsung).
### Research
Research showed that innovative performance depends on two factors:
- Relatedness
- Opportunity (value)

Both relatedness as opportunity increase innovative performance, but the combination of the two is the best.
## Success
When the [[Startup|Start-up]] successfully innovates, the corporation has two options:
1. Set-up an own firm with this innovation.
2. Acquire the [[Startup|Start-up]] (bad rep)
# Setting up
There are three steps to successfully setting up a [[Corporate venturing unit]]:
1. **Learn from experts**: hire external investment managers, participate in [[Venture capital]].
2. **Do it yourself**: set up clear strategic focus and reputation
3. **Venture at full speed**: secure internal venture funds, set up [[Joint venturing fund]] with non-rivals.
# Start-up perspective
[[Corporate venturing unit|Corporate venturing]] increases the success rate of [[Startup|Start-up]]s. This effect is enhanced if the two have relatedness.

However, start-ups might worry that the corporation learns to much and becomes a competitor.
# Research
