An [[Idea challenge]] is a challenge that a firm creates for their employees to generate ideas that can innovate the company. It allows the company to practice [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]].

[[Idea challenge|Idea challenges]] require three things:
1. High quantity of ideas
2. Selection of good ideas
3. Feedback
# High quantity of ideas
Research has shown that good [[Idea challenge]] models require three things:
1. Open challenge formulation (no specific needed innovation)
2. Good feedback from moderators
3. **EITHER** promotional communication of the challenge to employees **OR** good rewards
# Selection of good ideas
The selection process has to be efficient (fast and cheap) but also accurate. A trade-off exists between the two.

To combat this trade-off, companies can work with three rounds that each have a different balance between **efficiency** and **accuracy**:
- **Round 1**: stress efficiency (elevator pitches)
- **Round 2**: balance efficiency and accuracy ([[Business model canvas]] of the idea)
- **Round 3**: stress accuracy (short [[Business plan]] and presentation)
# Feedback
Feedback is crucial for the generation and development of good ideas. Additionally, feedback overlap (several people give similar feedback) has shown to improve the success of the idea.
