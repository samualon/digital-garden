Plug and Play is an accelerator (20 years old), provides several services:
- Match corporate sponsors and start-ups: big corporations can test technology of the [[Startup|Start-up]].
- Mentoring of the [[Startup|Start-up]]s (workshops, training)
- Possible equity stake
- Working space

Unexperienced [[Startup|Start-up]]s benefit the most from Plug and Play. 

Plug and Play also offers benefits to the corporations that join:
- Insights into new technologies, [[Sensing network]].
- Access to test technologies of these [[Startup|Start-up]]s (prototypes)

Corporations can choose between setting up their own [[Accelerator]]/[[Incubator]] or join Plug and Play, each have benefits:

| Own unit                                            | Plug and Play                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| More exclusive access to the [[Startup\|Start-up]]s | Matching                                                |
|                                                     | Access to more [[Startup\|Start-up]]s (broader network) |

Plug and Play earns money through:
- Fees from corporations
- Fees from [[Startup|Start-up]]s
- Revenue streams through equity stakes in [[Startup|Start-up]]s
