Alcatel-Lucent is between advocate and producer [[Intrapreneurial environment#Organizational ownership and resources|model]]. Started doing bootcamps in Belgium, then to the rest of the world. Needed to leverage the creativeness of the engineers.

Bootcamps = offer engineers that have an idea and take them off-site and let them work with business schools and teach them business models and create pitch for their ideas so that they can pitch to their managers.

Their bootcamps have strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths                                                         | Weaknesses                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| They create multifunctional teams                                 | Management support isn't guaranteed (after their pitch, there's no pot of funding) |
| Support from business schools                                     | Internal focus (only employees)                                                    |
| Open to all employees                                             | Way forward for employees = what to do after failed cases?                         |
| Low cost                                                          |                                                                                    |
| Early signs of success (some ideas have been implemented already) |                                                                                    |
