# Organizational ownership and resources
![[Organizational ownership and resource authority.png]]

Four models exist to classify intrapreneurship in a company. They depend on two dimensions:
1. **Organizational ownership**: measures how broad the focus of the group of intrapreneurs is.
2. **Resource authority**: measures the availability of provisioned funds for intrapreneurship.

These two dimensions result in four models:
1. Opportunist model
2. Enabler model
3. Advocate model
4. Producer model

Overall, the advocate and producer model are the best.
## Opportunist model
Everyone is supported for intrapreneurship, but there's no dedicated funding. This type of model might not lead to desired levels of [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] as not everyone is entrepreneurial.
## Enabler model
Everyone is supported and the company provides funding for [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]]. This however comes with disadvantages:
- Expensive
- Not everyone is entrepreneurial

An example was the 20% rule of [[Google]]. This was expensive however, as they had to pay for the 20% of labor even though no work for the company itself was done.
## Advocate model
There's a select group of intrapreneurs, but there's no dedicated funding. These intrapreneurs need to convince managers of their idea to receive funding.
## Producer model
There's a select group of intrapreneurs who have access to dedicated [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurial]] funding.

# Building and office design
Design of the company buildings has an impact on communication between employees, which impacts:
- Coordination (in projects and work)
- Information (inform other departments)
- **Inspiration (promoting creativity through each other)**

The latter is crucial and depends on *chance encounters* between employees.
## Impact of distance
Research has shown that separation / distance of employees heavily decreases *inspirational conversations*. For this reason, lineair designs should be avoided, as everyone is further from each other.

Moreover, vertical separation (over different floors of the building) is even more detrimental than horizontal separation.
## Open office design
Open offices have a negative effect on well-being, partly caused by a lack of privacy. Additionally, there's conflicting research on whether an open office increases communication.

Thus, an open office design isn't necessarily the best option.
## Personalized office
Being able to customize and personalize your office space has a positive impact on well-being and can thus increase creativity (at the cost of clean desk policies and professional appearance).
# HRM practices and incentives
![[HRM practices and incentives for intrapreneurship.png]]

HRM can be used in different ways to create an entrepreneurial environment in the firm:
1. Recruitment and selection
2. Job planning and design
3. Training and development
4. Performance appraisal
5. Compensation and rewards
## Recruitment and selection
By focusing recruitment on entrepreneurial profiles (candidates with entrepreneurial personality traits) and by relying on external and internal candidates, the company can increase [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]].
## Job planning and design
By focusing on the result and giving people autonomy in the execution, [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] is fostered.
## Training and development
A company can practice trend-watching and organize intrapreneurial events to promote [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]].
## Performance appraisal
By giving better performance appraisal to people that take risks, an entrepreneurial environment is created.
## Compensation and rewards
Firms can incentive [[Corporate entrepreneurship|Intrapreneurship]] by offering compensation and non-pecuniary rewards (f.e. entrepreneur of the month).
$$ Reward \rightarrow Motivation \rightarrow Effort \rightarrow Succesful \: performance \rightarrow Reward$$
### Principles of good reward programs
A good rewards program follows five principles:
1. Recognize failure
2. Deliver positive recognition publicly
3. Reward close to when the achievement is realized
4. Communicate clearly between achievement and award
5. Follow up on recognition of the achievements. Reinforce in meetings and newsletters.
