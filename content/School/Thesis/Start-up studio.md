A start-up studio is a new type of incubator that builds [[Startup]]s in a sustainable and repeatable way, using:
- in-house resources
- ideation through own network
- an internal team to develop the [[Startup]]s

The structure of these studios depends on their expertise and type of founders.

The start-up studio model is a type of [[Parallel entrepreneurship]].
# Characteristics
Start-up studios can be identified using 8 characteristics:
- Founders
- Ideation
- Equity
- Team
- Operations
- Exit
- Revenue source
## Founders
Founders of start-up studios are often experienced [[Serial entrepreneur]]s, since they have high willingness in the investment and the working on the development of a start-up.
## Ideation
Start-up studios are highly dependent on internal idea generation. However, more and more studios also generate outside ideas by acquiring them or co-founding.
## Equity
Since studios provide the most human and monetary capital to the new projects, they keep a majority stake in these start-ups.
## Team
Studio teams are highly volatile, they acquire members from different fields and with different expertises. 

Often, there are two teams. The core team manages the studio and its infrastructure. The specialized team is dedicated to individual projects.

These specialized teams are often recruiter as needed per project.
## Operations
Studios try to find an efficient and effective process of creating these start-ups to maximize success and speed. They focus on consistency.

Although all studios prioritize the latter, they use different strategies.
## Exit
Typically studios want their start-ups to get an acquired or reach an [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]] fast. This way they generate enough cash to start the next project.

[[Initial Public Offering|IPO]]s are difficult to reach for smaller studios or studios in a smaller economic environment. They will often just reach acquisition or spin-off.
# Team set-up
To avoid rookie mistakes during the start-up process, a team (or members) with entrepreneurial experience is crucial

Two approaches exist in team structure for start-up studios:
1. One multidisciplinary team that is divided over the projects conform the needs of the projects.
2. Recruitment per project. For every project, fitting people are recruited, plus some promotors. These promotors are the leaders of the start-up and receive a small percentage of equity as an incentive.
# Distinction with traditional incubators
There are five major aspects of differentiation between [[Start-up studio]] and [[Incubator]]s:
1. Equity
2. Team
3. Exit
4. Process
5. Simultaneous creation
## Equity
[[Start-up studio]] keep a majority stake in the [[Startup]]s, since the idea generation is mostly an internal process.

Sometimes, however, they accept external ideas, but they still demand much equity since they use mostly their own resources. 
## Team
Often, there are two teams. The core team manages the studio and its infrastructure. The specialized team is dedicated to individual projects.

These specialized teams are often recruiter as needed per project.
## Exit
For start-up studios, their main goal is to reach an exit through acquisition. For them, it's their main priority as they look to generate profit for [[Shareholders]].

Spin-offs, however, are possible.
## Process
The methodology adapted by start-up studios has a focus on:
- quickly adapting
- agility
- fast discarding in case of unsuccessfulness
## Simultaneous creation
Start-up studios create firms in a parallel fashion.
