# Summary
## Description
Lab Box is focusing on the future of mobility with the ambition to develop new solutions that make mobility seamless and sustainable again and support them in their growth.
## Companies
Lab Box both acquires small companies (5-6 members) and creates [[Startup]]s.
### Checks and balances
On a monthly basis, Lab Box core employees will hold Business review meetings with the different companies to asses 
- Financials
- Sales
- Market trends
- Pipeline
- Team
- Costs

The accounting is also checked by core financial members at Lab Box.

Additionally they meet every week with the [[Chief executive officer|CEO]]s of these companies to assess the biggest challenges and provide solutions.

They actively coach them.

In case that one of these pilars isn't up to par for one of these companies, Lab Box will decompose it to pinpoint what the cause is of this under-performance. Then the person responsible will receive additional training and support.
## Teams
### Members
Startup teams have 4 core members:
- [[Chief executive officer|CEO]]
- [[Chief financial officer|CFO]]
- [[Chief marketing officer|CMO]]
- [[Chief technology officer|CTO]]

These are the hardest profiles to find, as they require lots of diverse skills and experience. Often, these members are young and need to acquire experience fast.

When companies get bought by Lab Box, most of the time these four members are already present in the company.
### Diversity
Lab Box tries to attract diverse profiles by including diversity in the core team. A [[Chief executive officer|CEO]] with a foreign background will make it easier to attract other diverse profiles, as it shows that it's possible for these people to succeed.

Having women as [[Chief executive officer|CEO]] or co-CEO has shown to have impact.
### Recruitment
Depending on the project and its environment, some of these four profiles are more crucial.

Recruitment mostly happens for the four profiles above. These people then search and recruit the other needed members.
### Selection
#### Process
Lab Box will do extensive interviews and even use external parties to assess the skills and profile of possible recruits.

They test on different dimensions, intelligence as well as emotional intelligence.
#### Criteria
- Passion for the business
- Pragmatic

Lab Box searches for recruits with a fiery passion for the industry, a *change the world* attitude. The work can be heavy and stressful, thus this motivation is absolutely crucial.

A solution-based approach is also important. They have to be able to be very pragmatic in their decision-making.

Prior industry-specific experience can be a plus, but coming from another industry can also be benficial as they can apply trends from one industry to another ([[Entrepreneurship by Lazear]]).

People are also comfortable in different sizes of companies, 0 to 1, 1 to 10, 10 to 1000 and 1000 to infinity. You have to ensure that the company size fits the person's desired environment.
### Incentives
There are four tools to attract members to these projects:
- Project attractiveness
- Daily rate
- End-of-year bonus
- Long-term performance dependent bonuses

The latter, long-term incentives, are the most impacting.
### Network
The four core members typically fall back on their personal networks to find other needed members for the venture. The entrepreneurial space is a very closely connected environment, so finding these members mostly happens through networking.

They also receive job applications as they've grown in popularity.
