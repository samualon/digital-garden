[[Grounded theory]] aims to build a new theory based on a qualitative data set. It starts from the ground up, so hypotheses aren't made. It tries to discover a theoretical framework bottom-up.
# Method
The method has several steps:
1. Collect data on a small sample group
2. Create a theory based on the collected data
3. Test the theory on a second small sample group
4. Adjust the theory if needed
5. Repeat...
