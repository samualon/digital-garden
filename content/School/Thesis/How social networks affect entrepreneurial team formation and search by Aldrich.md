**Title**: SMALL WORLDS, INFINITE POSSIBILITIES? HOW SOCIAL NETWORKS AFFECT ENTREPRENEURIAL TEAM FORMATION AND SEARCH
**Author**: HOWARD E. ALDRICH and PHILLIP H. KIM
**Year**: 2007
**Research question**: How do social networks affect entrepreneurial team formation and search?
# Abstract
Social theorists offer two possible perspectives of networks of a person:
1. Infinite possibilities for someone to develop heterogenous networks
2. Constraint possibilities only for privileged individuals

The paper describes three models of network formation:
1. Random
2. Small world
3. Truncated scale free
# Content
## Introduction
Social networks play a role for who becomes an entrepreneur and successful. Understanding these networks is thus of importance.
## Network functionality
### Social networks in startups
Teams perform better than solo entrepreneurs, since they offer more instruments and social-psychological benefits.

Also, founders that don't have access to certain resources through their team, turn to indirect ties (such as pre-existing networks).
### Team formation
There are two formation strategies:
1. [[Resource-seeking team formation]]
2. [[Interpersonal attraction team formation]]
## Social network emergence
Three models for networks:
1. [[Random networks]]
2. [[Small world networks]]
3. [[Scale free network]]
