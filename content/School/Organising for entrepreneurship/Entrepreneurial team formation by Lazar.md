**Author**: Moran Lazar
**Year**: 2020
**Research question**:
# Abstract
The paper discusses the importance of entrepreneurial team formation for success but notes fragmentation in research due to different perspectives and contexts. It presents a framework analyzing why, how, when, and where teams form, highlighting the need for integration across disciplines and contexts. It emphasizes considering self-selection and endogeneity in founding characteristics. Overall, it suggests that more comprehensive exploration is needed to unlock the full potential of entrepreneurial team formation research.
