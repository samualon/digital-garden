An Initial Public Offering, or IPO, is the first time the shares of a private corporation are made available to the public through the issuance of a new stock.

For a good exit, the company should be in a phase where it look as attractive and valuable as possible.
# Preparation
To prepare for the process of an [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]], the company needs to:
- be a fully-fledged independent company
- satisfy listing requirements
- find an investment bank to manage the process
# Process
The [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]] process consists of:
1. Selecting an investment bank
2. Pricing
3. Structuring

![[IPO process.png]]
## Selecting an investment bank
A good investment bank:
- Manages the whole listing process
- Ensures demands are met
- Do [[Due diligence]]
- Prepare a *road show*
- Price the [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]]
## Pricing
Three possibilities exist for pricing [[Initial Public Offering|IPO]] shares:
- Book-building
- Fixed-price
- Auction
## Structuring
The company has to think about:
- their primary, secondary and free float shares.
- the type of shares to sell
# Advantages and disadvantages
| Advantage                                      | Disadvantage                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Growth opportunity                             | Costs time and money                                                                           |
| Increased visibility                           | Disclosure (regulatory financial updates to the public)                                        |
| Possibility to sell shares at overvalued price | Reliant on market conditions                                                                   |
| Liquidity for [[Shareholders]]                 | Possible underpricing (investors correct for overvalueing)                                     |
|                                                | Increased transparancy to competitors and [[Shareholders]] through disclosure (annual reports) |
