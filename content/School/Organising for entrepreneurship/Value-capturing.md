By selling products and services at a price above the cost of production and below the [[Willingness to pay]] of customers, a company is able to capture the [[Value-creation|value created]].

This value contributes to the [[Enterprise value]].
# Determining value-captured
To identify fundamental indicators of value in a firm, one should only look at operating incomes and expenses (thus excluding other forms of income or expenses).

The fundamental indicators of value are:
- Revenue/sales
- [[EBITDA]]
- [[EBIT]]
- [[NOPAT]]
- [[NOPLAT]]
