[[Economic value added|EVA]] is a measure of a firm's financial performance. It uses the [[NOPAT]], invested capital and the [[Weighted average cost of capital|WACC]]. 

$$ EVA = NOPAT - (Invested \: capital * WACC) $$
With $Invested \: capital = Equity + Debt$.
