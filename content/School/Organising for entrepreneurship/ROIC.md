Return On Invested Capital calculates the return on [[Invested capital]] using [[NOPLAT]]:
$$ ROIC = \frac{NOPLAT}{Invested \: capital} $$
This formula can be broken down into a factor of two important components of [[Enterprise value]], margin and resource utilization
$$ ROIC = \frac{NOPLAT}{Sales} * \frac{Sales}{Invested \: capital} $$
With margin $=\frac{NOPLAT}{Sales}$ and resource utilization $=\frac{Sales}{Invested \: capital}$.
