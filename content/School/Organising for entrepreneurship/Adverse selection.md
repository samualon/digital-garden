# External financing
## Risky and safe firms
Banks know the general distribution of risk carried by firms, but don't know the individual risk for an individual firm. They can only make assumptions about firms with safe and risky projects:
- Safe firms have high chance of success but low returns.
- Risky firms have low chance of success but high returns.
## Profit maximization
Banks want to maximize their profits and thus their intrest rate. The problem is that only risky firms will accept very high rates. This creates adverse selection.
## Problem
Profits for banks thus increases by an increase of intrest rate **up to a point where safe firms start to drop out**. From this point on bank profit will decrease with increasing rates.

Banks will thus not lend beyond a certain intrest rate, even though there is demand, which creates [[Credit rationing]].
