Your Business model is the whole of your business in a nutshell. It talks about the:
- value-creation = creating a valuable product or service
- value-delivery = delivering this value to the customer
- value-capturing = make money from the product or service

The business model indicates how [[Value-creation]] and a [[Competitive advantage]] are related to the [[Value-creation]] of the firm.

[[Value-creation and value-capturing]] have a relation in terms of [[Willingness to pay]].

There are methods to [[Product-service operations plan|develop an effective business model]].
# Choices
A business model consists of choices that management has made:
- Policy choices
- Asset choices
- Government choices

It also focuses on the consequences of these choices. They should generate a *virtuous cycle* of feed-back loops that are self-reinforcing.
