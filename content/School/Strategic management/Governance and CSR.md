# Purpose of CSR
[[CR17 The Social Responsibility of Business Is to Increase Its Profits|CR17]] states that the only long term purpose of a firm is profits.

CEO of BlackRock contradicts this, saying profits is not the only purpose since it can drive profits and ethical behavior in the long run.

Patagonia's CEO says that societal purpose is possible for a firm to execute as long as a firm is profitable.
## Shared value
![[Purpose in phase 2.png]]

Purpose (as vision, mission and values) drives a firm's strategy.

For long, firms have been seen as the driver of societal problems, *a firm can't make profit without causing societal problems*.

In reality, as societal problems cause important needs, business can profit from solving these problems. Porter named this [[Shared value]]. 
# Firm strategy and CSR
## The trade-off
A company can choose to maximize profits while abiding to the minimum legal standard, **OR** maximize social outcomes while abiding to the cost of capital. 

![[CSR and profits.png]]
## Win-win
However, some [[Corporate sustainability and responsibility|CSR]] such as shared value in [[CR18 Creating Shared Value]], could increase social outcomes while also increasing profitability of the firm.

Some societal activities could complement corporate activities, resulting in a *win-win*. To move to such a *win-win* situation, three things are needed:
- *Contextualize*: 
  think of the context of the firm, in terms of geography, industry, etc.
- *Cooperate*: 
  with other players
- *Innovate*
