# Threats
The sustainability of a competitive advantage might be threatened either through its [[Economic value added|Added economic value]] or its [[Value-capturing]]. There exist four threats:
- [[Imitation threat]]
- [[Substitution threat]]
- [[Holdup threat]]
- [[Slack threat]]
