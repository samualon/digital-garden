# Categories
[[Firm resources]] and [[Firm capabilities]] can be categorized into three groups:
- [[Tangible resources]] or capabilities
- [[Intangible resources]] or capabilities
- Organizational, such as productivity, cross-functional integrations, knowledge sharing, ...

![[Types of resources and capabilities.png]]
## Value chain
Each part of the value chain requires different types of capabilities. The [[Basic capabilities]] are directly related to parts of the [[Value chain]]. *[[Integrative and directive capabilities]]* are required to support steer and integrate all capabilities.
# Dimensions
[[Basic capabilities]] have three sequential dimensions:

| Step | Dimension  | Example                  |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------------------ |
| 1    | Activity   | New product introduction |
| 2    | Technology | Access to technology     |
| 3    | Skills     | Project management       |
| 4    | Resources  | Budget                   |
| 5    | Result     | Time to market           |
# Developing capabilities
Ideas for capabilities can be sourced using:
- Customer contact
- Conditions related to business location
- Transfering trends from related industries
- In-house experience

These can be developed by:
- Systematic research
- Learning by doing
- [[Leapfrogging]] investments in resources
- Innovatively combining resources, skills and technologies
