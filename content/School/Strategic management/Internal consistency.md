To ensure [[Internal consistency]], all elements of a strategy should fit well together. Three parts to this topic exist:
1. Does scope match activities, resources and capabilities?
2. Do activities reinforce the [[Competitive advantage]]?
3. Are [[Resources and capabilities]] that reinforce the [[Competitive advantage]] being developed?
# Scope and activities, resources and capabilities
Your activities, resources and capabilities should match the scope of your firm.
