# Preparation questions
1. What are the strategies of Netflix and Disney in the market for streamed video entertainment?
2. What resources and capabilities are needed to compete within this industry? In relation to these resources and capabilities, assess the positions of both Netflix and Disney.
3. To what extent are the different strategies of Netflix and Disney in the market for streamed video entertainment reflections of the different resource/capability profiles of the two companies? How effectively does each company’s strategy exploit its key strengths while protecting itself from its key weaknesses?
4. Which company do you think will be more successful in the global market for streamed video entertainment? What recommendations would you offer to either Netflix or Disney?
# Attempt
## Employed strategies
### Netflix
- Creating original content
- Step-by-step expansion
- Localized versions of US shows
- Creating local content
- Personalized content through learning algorithms
- Create shows using preferences from user data
- High performance requirements from staff
- Partnerships with Roku, XBOX, etc.
### Disney
- Acquire content trough [[Mergers and acquisitions]]
- Create full franchises from movies
- Investing in animation technology
- Partnerships with media and communication companies
- Advertisement in service (Hulu)
## Needed resources and capabilities
1. Content
2. Distribution channels
3. User interface
### Netflix
1. Netflix invests heavily in creating original content.
2. Netflix tries to incorporate its service into other products, making it more accessible.
3. Netflix entertains a workforce which focuses on taking risks and high performance, trying to innovate their interface and increase personalization of content.
### Disney
1. Disney acquires companies that have lots of content (Lucas films, 21st century Fox, etc.)
2. Bought a company that has the technology to do online streaming, not perfect.
3. Doesn't invest in innovative UI
