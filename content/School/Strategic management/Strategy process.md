# Strategic thinking and execution
When a firm has developed a strategy, it has to be turned into concrete actions and an execution. This is a different way of thinking and working.

![[Strategic thinking and execution.png]]

- **Strategic thinking**: develop new ideas and directions
- **Strategic execution**: translating ideas into actions to the people of the firm.
# Management system
The closed [[Closed-loop management system]] offers a way to execute strategy.

![[Closed-loop management system.png]]

Using the levers of the strategy, a scope needs to defined, activities put in place and [[Resources and capabilities]] provided to execute these activities.

![[Levers, scope, activities, resources and capabilities.png]]
# Impact of management
Research has shown that *upper echelon* management (the CEO, etc.) has a big impact on the performance of a firm (positive or negative).
## Biases in decision-making
Biases are possible in the execution of a strategy:
- [[Overconfidence]] and [[Over-optimism]]
- [[Confirmation bias]] and [[Group think]]
- [[Loss aversion]]
- [[Status quo bias]] and [[Preference for even allocation]]
- [[Sunk-cost fallacy]]
# Networks
## Impact of networks
Your network can have a big impact on your performance.
