# The role of resources and capabilities in strategy
Two factors increased the focus of resources and capabilities in strategy:
- Firms' environments have become less stable. For this reason internal resources and capabilities are more secure for strategy than external market offers.
- Competitive advantage is a bigger source of profitability than industry attractiveness.
## Basing strategy on resources and capabilities
A *resource-based view* of the firm was becoming more mainstream in strategy. It conceptualizes the company as a set of resources and capabilities that form the foundation of the competitive advantage and strategy.

This is a more secure view. Answering the question *"What is our business*" with who your customers are is less secure since customer preferences are becoming more volatile.
## Profit from resources and capabilities
Industry factors compose a smaller portion of profits, competitive advantage has a bigger impact. Focusing on resources and capabilities, which supports competitive advantage, is a better strategy approach.

Profits of a company can arise from two types of *rent*:
- [[Monopoly rents]]
- [[Ricardian rents]]

Industry attractiveness and barriers of entry of [[Porter's five forces]], suggest that industry attractiveness results from superior resources.

Creating a competitive advantage thus requires a strategy that exploits a firm's unique strengths.
