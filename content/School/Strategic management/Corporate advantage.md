The [[Competitive advantage]] that a company gains by having multiple [[Strategic business unit|SBU]]s work together.

A corporation is said to have a [[Corporate advantage]] if it has driven a *wedge* between the break-up value of its businesses (all the [[Strategic business unit|SBU]]s seperately) and the [[Enterprise value]] (the businesses as a corporate whole).
